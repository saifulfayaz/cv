About Me

I am a final-year Electrical Engineering student at the University of Kashmir, on track to graduate in 2026. My academic journey has been driven by a genuine passion for electrical systems, energy infrastructure, and the principles that power modern society.

Beyond my core engineering programme, I hold an NCVT-certified COPA diploma from ITI Bandipora — providing practical grounding in computing and IT that meaningfully complements my technical background.

An internship at the Kishanganga Hydroelectric Power Station under NHPC Limited was one of the most formative experiences of my education. Working inside a live utility environment gave me firsthand exposure to large-scale power generation, high-voltage substations, and grid-level operations — translating coursework into practical mastery of complex energy infrastructure. I come from Bandipora, a district with remarkable renewable energy potential, and I am committed to contributing to its future.
